# Request storage

This guide will cover how to request storage in your workspace.

> **Note:** You can only request cloud resources once you have completed your workspace metadata.

1. Navigate to your workspace.

1. Click on the `Toolbox` button in the workspace sidebar.
    ![resource list](/api/docs/UserGuide/GettingStarted/updated_toolbox-sidebar.png)

1. Click on the `Add` button next to the resource(s) you want to provision. They will appear in a list in the Summary tab.
    ![request storage](/api/docs/UserGuide/GettingStarted/updated_resource-list.png)

1. Once you are done adding the resources you want to provision, click on the `Next step` button at the bottom of the page.

1. Review the summary of the tool(s) you want to provision. If you are satisfied, click on the `Complete request` button.
